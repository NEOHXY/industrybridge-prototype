About this challenge

Malaysian factories lose time and material to defects caught too late on the line. Over four weeks your team learns the industry context, interviews operators, and builds an AI-assisted prototype that spots defects earlier. Finalists pilot their solution with a partner factory in Kuala Lumpur.
